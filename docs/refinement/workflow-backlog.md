## Contracts

`GET /api/v1/pantry/summary` retains filtered `batches`, while `productStock` is household active stock per returned product across display filters. Each entry contains `knownQuantity`, its `unit`, and `uncertain`. It excludes inactive and grocery-excluded batches, converts only compatible known units, and never turns unavailable conversion evidence into a confirmed shortage. No stock is modified by browsing.

Ingredient food-link review remains a UI confirmation of semantic identity; quantities and Pantry stock remain separate. This check applies to normal submit and keyboard form submit, regardless of focus or blur. Saved recipe/domain validation remains authoritative.

An uncertain meal addition remains blocked even if the local selection changes. Refreshing and reviewing the current calendar remains the recovery route; the selector does not claim server idempotency.
